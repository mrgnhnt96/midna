## L1 · lists · 2026-11-09
- **Rule:** A section label inside a list (time dividers, group headers) must never share a row title's size, weight, colour or start edge. Make it a different kind of text: small uppercase in `dim`, starting at the row's leading edge (the status dot), not the name column, with a rule or band behind it.
- **Why:** In the Summary list a "Last hour 3" label in the row-name style read as just another terminal row ("looks just like the title of a tab. Very confusing").
- **Applies to:** all
- **From:** the owner's correction on the Summary time dividers canvas
